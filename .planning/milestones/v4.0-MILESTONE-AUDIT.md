## Architectural Improvements

### 1. Pure Single-Database Architecture (`streaming.duckdb`)
- **Before**: The engine spliced Polygon/MASSIVE 1-minute historical bars from `historical.duckdb` with raw ticks from `streaming.duckdb`, leading to Frankenstein price mismatches, timestamp discrepancies, and garbled chart rendering.
- **After**: `historical.duckdb` is completely purged from configuration, backend Python code, and schema validation. All candles (sub-second to 1D) are dynamically synthesized from raw Databento/Capital.com ticks in `streaming.duckdb` via DuckDB's vectorized `time_bucket()` in 26 milliseconds.

### 2. Event-Driven Playback Aggregation & Volume Deduplication
- **Before**: The chart subscriber naively checked `currentTick` and compounded volume on clock tick advances even when no new trade occurred, while missing intermediate high/low spikes when multiple ticks elapsed per animation frame. Premarket fallback ticks repeatedly added whole-minute volume.
- **After**: `useChartLifecycle` tracks `lastConsumedTickRef` and `lastConsumedTimeRef`, aggregating all elapsed trades in the frame into exact `high`, `low`, `close`, and summed volume. Clock updates without new trades never increment volume, and fallback ticks with `isSynthesized: true` are capped.

### 3. Canvas Lifecycle Reconciliation & Symbol Isolation
- **Before**: Arriving historical prefixes sharing the same end timestamp were skipped as "incremental" updates. Switching symbols retained old ticker prices if the date range matched due to array equality shortcuts.
- **After**: Prepending detection forces `setData()` when older bars arrive. Ticker switching compares prices and tracks `loadedTickerRef` to immediately flush old candles and render the new symbol's data.

### 4. Monotonic Session Generation Protection
- **Before**: Changing dates triggered asynchronous fetches that could resolve out of order, overwriting the new session with stale historical data.
- **After**: Monotonic `sessionGenRef` tokens reject out-of-order responses, ensuring older requests never rewind or overwrite the active session.

### 5. Multi-Symbol Time & Sales Tape Isolation
- **Before**: Time & Sales windowed the raw unified tick buffer, mixing TSLA, NVDA, and SPY trades together with false price uptick/downtick calculations.
- **After**: Executed trades are strictly filtered by the active chart's symbol badge, with upticks/downticks calculated solely relative to preceding trades of the same asset.

### 6. Timeline Scrubber Stabilization & Exact Seeking
- **Before**: Scrubber domain floated with loaded tick ranges and drifted with fractional milliseconds.
- **After**: Slider bounds and seeks snap to integer seconds with zero millisecond drift, immediate pause on seek, and explicit `HH:MM:SS` jump input.
