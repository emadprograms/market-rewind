## Architectural & Transition Hardening

### 1. React Hook-Order Safety in Time & Sales (`TimeAndSales.tsx`)
- **Defect (Probe 1):** `usePlaybackStore` subscriptions were located below early return `if (!isOpen) return null`, throwing `Rendered more hooks than during previous render` when toggling the tape drawer.
- **Hardening:** Store subscriptions moved to the unconditional top-level sequence. Verified via closed->open rerender unit tests and Playwright drawer lifecycle testing.

### 2. Temporal State Machine Synchronization (`useChartLifecycle.ts`)
- **Defect (Probes 2 & 3):** `lastConsumedTimeRef` was initialized to 0 and only advanced during forward play. Seeking forward left it at 0, doubling volume on the first play frame. Rewinding left it at a future time, completely suppressing subsequent trades up to the previously visited timestamp.
- **Hardening:** 
  - `lastConsumedTimeRef` is initialized to `state.currentTime` on mount and synchronized to `chartData` snapshot updates.
  - Rewind discontinuity detection lowers `lastConsumedTimeRef` to `state.currentTime` and clears `lastConsumedTickRef`, ensuring intermediate high/low/volume trades aggregate correctly without suppression.
  - Linear tick array scans were replaced with O(log N) binary search (`findFirstTickAfter`) and early loop break upon exceeding `state.currentTime`.

### 3. Forming Candle Interval Protection (`useChartData.ts`)
- **Defect (Probe 5):** Look-ahead protection previously tested timestamp equality (`bMs === effectiveCutoff`). At 1 second past boundary (09:20:01), it failed the check and leaked the completed 5-minute candle high (150 vs currently known 101).
- **Hardening:** Replaced equality check with interval containment: `bMs <= effectiveCutoff && bMs + 60000 > effectiveCutoff`, enforcing absolute temporal isolation across the entire forming candle lifespan.

### 4. Multi-Minute Fallback Constituent Volume (`useChartLifecycle.ts`)
- **Defect (Probe 4):** Higher timeframe synthetic candles replaced volume with only the latest minute (`lastCandle.volume = tickVol`), discarding preceding constituent minutes (e.g. 1000 shares + 200 shares fell to 200 shares).
- **Hardening:** Introduced `syntheticBucketVolumesRef` mapping each constituent minute within the bucket to its volume. The higher timeframe candle volume reflects the sum of all constituent minutes.

### 5. Session Tick Loader Race Prevention (`src/App.tsx`)
- **Defect (Probe 7):** When switching session dates, asynchronous tick fetches lacked generation checks. If a request for September 22 finished after a switch to September 15, the active session time was erroneously reset to September 22.
- **Hardening:** Monotonic `sessionGenRef` introduced to `loadStreamingTicks`. Responses with mismatched generation IDs are discarded immediately.

### 6. Scrubber Premarket Domain Persistence (`src/components/PlaybackBar.tsx`)
- **Defect (Probe 6):** With session start at 09:20 ET and first tick at 09:30 ET, seeking forward to 09:34 evaluated `Math.min(09:30, 09:34) = 09:30`, shifting the slider minimum forward and clipping premarket hours.
- **Hardening:** `sessionBoundsRef` anchors minimum and maximum bounds to the established session start. Seeking forward preserves the 09:20 minimum domain while expanding if earlier times are reached.
