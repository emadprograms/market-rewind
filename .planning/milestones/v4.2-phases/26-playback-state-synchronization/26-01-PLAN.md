## 1. Objectives & Scope

1. **REV-SYNC-01 (Hook Order)**:
   - Move `usePlaybackStore` selector for `latestTickBySymbol` in `src/components/TimeAndSales.tsx` unconditionally to top level before `if (!isOpen) return null`.
   - Ensure opening/closing the Time and Sales panel never alters the number of hooks called or causes React hook-order errors.

2. **REV-SYNC-02 (Post-Seek Volume Doubling)**:
   - In `src/hooks/useChartLifecycle.ts`, initialize `lastConsumedTimeRef` to the active store `currentTime` (or last bar time) rather than 0.
   - When `chartData` updates (on mount, seek, symbol change, or timeframe switch), synchronize `lastConsumedTimeRef.current` with `currentTime` and `lastConsumedTickRef.current` with the latest consumed tick for the ticker.
   - When playback resumes after a seek, ensure ticks with timestamps `<= currentTime` that were already rendered into `chartData` are not re-processed.

3. **REV-SYNC-03 (Post-Rewind Trade Suppression)**:
   - In `src/hooks/useChartLifecycle.ts`, observe temporal discontinuities (backward seeking / rewinding) both while paused and during active playback.
   - When `state.currentTime < lastConsumedTimeRef.current` or when seeking/rewinding, update `lastConsumedTimeRef.current = state.currentTime` and reset `lastConsumedTickRef.current = null`.
   - Ensure replay after rewind captures all intermediate ticks and aggregates their high, low, and volume without omission.

4. **REV-SYNC-04 (O(log N) Binary-Search Tick Ingestion)**:
   - In `src/hooks/useChartLifecycle.ts`, replace the linear `for (let i = 0; i < symbolTicks.length; i++)` tick scan with a binary search locating the first candidate tick strictly greater than `lastConsumedTimeRef.current`.
   - Terminate the loop immediately when `tMs > state.currentTime` to avoid scanning through future ticks.

5. **Synthesized Tick Flagging**:
   - In `src/store/usePlaybackStore.ts`, ensure `isSynthesized: true` is included in all fallback tick synthesis paths within `seekTickTime`.
