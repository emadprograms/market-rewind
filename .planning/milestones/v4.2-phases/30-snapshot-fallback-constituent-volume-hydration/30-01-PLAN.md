**Phase:** 30 — Snapshot Fallback Constituent Volume Hydration
**Plan:** 01
**Requirements:** CONV-VOL-01, CONV-VOL-02

---

## 1. Objectives & Scope

1. **CONV-VOL-01 (Constituent Fallback Volume State Reconstruction)**:
   - In `src/hooks/useChartLifecycle.ts`, when hydrating a `chartData` snapshot, populate `syntheticBucketVolumesRef` with constituent minutes from `usePlaybackStore.getState().masterData` that started before `currentPlayback.currentTime`.
   - If `masterData` is unavailable or empty, preserve the snapshot's accumulated `lastCandleRef.current.volume` as an anchor minute so existing volume is not wiped.
   - Prevent volume from resetting to only the active minute when playback resumes after a seek.

2. **CONV-VOL-02 (Zero Double-Counting on Playback Resume)**:
   - In `useChartLifecycle.ts`, maintain minute-keyed map replacement (`minutes.set(minuteKey, tickVol)`).
   - Ensure subsequent ticks for the current minute update that minute's contribution rather than adding onto it or duplicating previous minutes.
