**Phase:** 27 — Temporal Isolation & Volume Accumulation

## 1. Objectives & Scope

1. **REV-FORM-01 (Forming Candle Look-Ahead Protection Across Entire Bucket)**:
   - In `src/hooks/useChartData.ts`, replace the exact instant check `bMs === effectiveCutoff` with the interval check `bMs <= effectiveCutoff && bMs + 60000 > effectiveCutoff`.
   - Ensure forming candles never expose completed constituent high/low/volume values at any second before bucket close (e.g. 1 second past boundary at 09:20:01).

2. **REV-FORM-02 (Multi-Minute Fallback Constituent Volume Accumulation)**:
   - In `src/hooks/useChartLifecycle.ts`, maintain `syntheticBucketVolumesRef` mapping constituent minute timestamps to their respective volume within the higher-timeframe bucket.
   - Accumulate constituent minute volumes instead of overwriting the entire higher-timeframe volume with only the latest minute.
   - Maintain DIAG 6 guarantee that repeated frames within the same minute update that constituent minute rather than compounding.

3. **REV-FORM-03 (Session Tick Loader Generation Guard in App.tsx)**:
   - In `src/App.tsx`, introduce `sessionGenRef` to `loadStreamingTicks`.
   - Increment generation token on every session/date/ticker transition and verify `currentGen === sessionGenRef.current` before committing `allTicks`, `targetMs`, and `currentTime`.
   - Prevent late tick responses from older dates from overwriting the active session.
