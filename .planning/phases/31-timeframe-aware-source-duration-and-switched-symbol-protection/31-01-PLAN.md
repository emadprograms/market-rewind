# Phase 31: Timeframe-Aware Source Duration & Switched Symbol Protection — Plan 01

**Phase:** 31 — Timeframe-Aware Source Duration & Switched Symbol Protection
**Plan:** 01
**Requirements:** CONV-TIME-01, CONV-TIME-02

---

## 1. Objectives & Scope

1. **CONV-TIME-01 (Source-Resolution Aware Forming Candle Protection)**:
   - In `src/hooks/useChartData.ts`, parameterize forming candle containment by `candidateDurationMs` rather than hardcoded 60 seconds (`60000`).
   - For `localMasterData` candidates, derive duration from the chart's timeframe resolution (`TF_SECONDS[timeframe] * 1000`), such as 300,000ms for 5min, 900,000ms for 15min, or 3,600,000ms for 1h.

2. **CONV-TIME-02 (Switched Symbol Local History Containment)**:
   - When switching symbols where global minute history is for another asset (e.g. AAPL) and candidate bars fall back to the selected symbol's local history (e.g. TSLA 5m), ensure unclosed bars do not leak future high, low, close, or volume at intermediate timestamps (such as 09:21).
   - Display safe opening-price placeholders (`{ open: b.open, high: b.open, low: b.open, close: b.open, volume: 0 }`) until the source bar end time has elapsed.
