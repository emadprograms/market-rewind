## 3. Discovered Technical Debt & Gaps for Milestone v3.0
1. **Mode Simplification**: The TICK vs. BAR toggle is confusing and unnecessary; users require strictly tick-by-tick replay.
2. **Temporal Isolation (Future Data Leak)**: Replay queries must be strictly bounded to the selected historical date so future data does not render.
3. **Canonical Date Reset**: Resetting or switching to any day must always jump to 9:20 AM ET of that day.
4. **Intraday Live Candle Updates on Higher Timeframes**: When playing on 5m, 15m, 30m, 1H charts, the forming candle must update its High, Low, Close, and Volume live with every incoming tick.
5. **Universal Tick Availability**: For dates where raw tick data is sparse or absent, micro-ticks must be synthesized from 1-minute historical candles so tick replay is 100% resilient for all 40 symbols and 500+ days.
